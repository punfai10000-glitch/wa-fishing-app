    Before you go fishing

    • Scores rate environmental conditions only. They are not a guarantee of fish or of safety.
    • Conditions change fast. Always check official warnings and look at the water yourself before fishing.
    • Rock fishing and spearfishing are dangerous. Wear a lifejacket on rocks, never fish or dive alone.
    • Official DPIRD fishing rules always prevail over anything shown here.
    • This is an early version: locations are still being verified and safety limits are drafts. Underwater visibility is an estimate, not a measurement.
